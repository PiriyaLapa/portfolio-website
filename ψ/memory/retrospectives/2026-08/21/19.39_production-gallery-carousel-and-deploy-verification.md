## Lessons Learned

- **A verification workflow that names a specific CLI/tool as its primary
  method should be treated as provisional per-environment, not durable
  across sessions — check the tool is actually present before relying on it,
  even (especially) right after you've just documented that workflow
  yourself.** Writing `deploy-verifier` and then hitting `vercel: command not
  found` on its very first real use is the sharpest possible reminder that a
  sandboxed dev environment's toolset can differ session-to-session even in
  the same repo. The fix isn't "always curl instead" — `vercel inspect` gave
  more deployment metadata when it worked in a prior session — it's "the
  documented primary step needs a presence check or an explicit
  environment-currency caveat," not an assumption that what worked once will
  keep working.
- **When a pasted UI/content mockup arrives, split it into "structural/layout
  idea" and "copy/data/images" as two separate judgment calls before deciding
  anything — never treat porting a mockup as one yes/no decision.** The
  layout idea (a carousel) and the content (fabricated narratives, fake
  status badges, placeholder images) came from the same paste but deserved
  completely different verdicts. Evaluating them as a single unit would have
  either shipped fabrication or thrown away a genuinely good design
  suggestion.
