## Lessons Learned

- **A term already being public on a site is not evidence it was approved
  for disclosure — it may just be an incomplete prior cleanup.** Before
  reusing "already-live" phrasing in new content, check whether there's a
  git history reason it might still be a live violation, not a green
  light.
- **When a CLI's embedded network client hardcodes `127.0.0.1` for a
  service actually running across a VM/container/WSL boundary, a local TCP
  proxy (plain socket forwarding, no special tooling needed) is a
  legitimate and fast fix** — don't assume you need root/sudo/installed
  packages to solve a loopback-vs-gateway-IP mismatch.
- **A background process producing literally zero output past its normal
  startup window is a stronger signal than a slow one** — silence isn't
  "still working," it's frequently "stuck on a step you can't see." Switch
  to verbose/diagnostic mode immediately rather than waiting longer on the
  same invocation.
